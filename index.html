Privacy Policy — English

1. Publisher and app

This policy applies to KAZE DRIFT (formerly titled KAZE VECTOR DRIFT in the app; Android package: com.kazevector.drift). Publisher: UFYTEGAME. Privacy contact: uguryiilmaz34@gmail.com.

2. Data stored locally

The game saves level progress, scores, records, virtual credits, unlocked fictional vehicles, upgrades, difficulty and audio/control preferences on your device through Android WebView localStorage. These are offline gameplay records. The game has no account registration or login.

3. Motion sensor

If you select tilt controls, the app reads gravity or accelerometer values solely for real-time steering on your device. These values are not permanently stored or transmitted. Swipe and on-screen buttons are available as alternatives.

4. Collection, sharing and third parties

The reviewed offline version 4.4.3 contains no ads, analytics, account system, payments or online leaderboards and does not request the INTERNET permission. The app does not transmit personal data or game progress to developer or third-party servers and does not sell such data. The game uses the Android system WebView component. Data practices of Google Play and Android system services are governed by their respective providers.

5. Optional export and import

You may voluntarily export or import your progress as a JSON file using the system file picker. If you choose to store that file with a third-party cloud or storage provider, further processing is subject to your choices and that provider’s policies.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Game data remains in the app’s local storage until you delete it. Settings → “Yerel kayıtları sil” lets you reset local progress after confirmation. You can also clear app data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Previously exported JSON files must be deleted separately. Local data benefits from standard Android app sandboxing. Android automatic app backup is disabled for this version.

7. Children and future updates

This version has no accounts, targeted advertising or online social features. Before adding analytics, ads, user accounts or online leaderboards, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declarations will be reviewed and updated.

8. Questions and changes

Contact us at uguryiilmaz34@gmail.com. We will publish policy updates on this page. Updated: November 17, 2026.

9. Contacting us by email

If you contact us by email voluntarily, we may use your message and email address to respond. Your email service provider and our email provider have their own privacy practices. This is separate from automated data collection by the game, which this version does not perform.
